Who we areREADY
LeTip Bridge Connections is a New York networking chapter built on one rule: one business category per seat, no competitors in the room. Eight members meet weekly to pass qualified referrals directly to each other — real estate, mortgage, real estate law, business funding, marketing, financial planning, personal injury law, and residential cleaning, all represented, none overlapping. The result is a referral network built on people who've actually vetted each other's work, not a directory of strangers. If you're a business owner in a category we don't already have filled, we'd like to meet you.
Meet the chapterREADY
Eight members, eight categories, one referral chapter: Drew Epstein (Residential Real Estate), Joseph Campagna (Mortgage Loans), Matthew Turturro (Real Estate Law), Daniel Wiener (Business Funding), Danielle Rizzo (Digital Marketing), Peter Arborea (Financial Planning), Rachel Kaylie (Personal Injury Law), and Ndeye Andon (Residential Cleaning). Between us, we cover most of what a person or business actually needs — buying a home, funding a business, or just getting found online. That coverage isn't an accident. It's the whole design.
Why a referral network worksREADY
Cold outreach gets you a maybe, if you're lucky enough to get a reply at all. A referral from someone your prospect already trusts gets you most of the way to a yes before you've said a word. That's the entire economics behind a structured referral chapter: it turns a slow, expensive sales problem into a warm handoff between people who've already vetted each other's work. It's a different sales motion, not just a networking event.
How to get connectedREADY
Need a real estate agent, a mortgage broker, help funding your business, or someone to finally get your company showing up on Google? One place, one short form, and we'll connect you directly with the right person in our network — not a general inbox, the actual person who handles what you need. Link in bio.
